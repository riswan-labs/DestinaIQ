## Launch controls

Mandatory support requirements are [FR-023](../product/REQUIREMENTS.md#fr-023), [FR-024](../product/REQUIREMENTS.md#fr-024), [FR-026](../product/REQUIREMENTS.md#fr-026), [FR-028](../product/REQUIREMENTS.md#fr-028), [FR-029](../product/REQUIREMENTS.md#fr-029). Provenance and temporal validity ship with profiles. Administration must review quarantined facts and track reports. Privacy includes export, deletion and isolation. Launch controls also cover the security, provenance, operations, coverage and quality gates in [traceability](../engineering/TRACEABILITY.md). NFR-13 applies at launch to free critical safety and independent rankings. Commercial scale approval follows later.

## Coverage and exclusions

Candidate markets are French Polynesia, Maldives, Seychelles, Mauritius, UAE, Singapore, Japan, Thailand, Indonesia, Egypt, Qatar, Turkey, Greece, Italy, Switzerland, France, United Kingdom, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and Mexico. All 20 await feasibility validation. Separate searchable and supported catalogs. Launch markets need licensed, fresh canonical geography, climate, forecasts, an official advisory authority, attractions, airport and transport data, and practical essentials. News can report zero verified recent items. Mark unsupported information unknown and publish category coverage. Exclude markets that fail mandatory coverage.

V1.0 includes sourced basic parking notes. Dedicated parking search, neighborhood risk assessments, incident maps, comparison of authority guidance, advanced atlas, simulator, mailbox access, paid flight feeds, Digital Twin, daily briefings, marketplace, native apps, predictive intelligence, document vault and community social features are post-launch. [Release assignments](REQUIREMENTS.md) govern all 56 post-launch FRs.
