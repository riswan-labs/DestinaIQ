# Customer discovery methodology

## Scope and classification

Observed 2026-11-27, America/Los_Angeles. Search public publisher and community pages for trip planning tool switching, best months, itinerary pacing, transfer/parking, prices, alerts and app alternatives. Search each topic for both friction and a workable counterexample. Example queries include `Japan itinerary too packed reddit`, `Oahu rental car parking reddit`, `TripIt flight delay alternative TravelHacks`, `French Polynesia Bora Bora weather shoulder season forum`, and `2025 visitor arrivals` scoped to the official statistician. Read the visible text, retain short paraphrases with thread-level URLs, and separate paid-app anecdotes from purchase intent. Search candidate markets by official visitor series and relevant journey detail. Deduplicate syndicated figures, repeat comments and commercially interested posts where possible. Record the source period and population before making comparisons. The accepted CP-0.2 benchmark contributes product context only.

Official statistical evidence supports published counts or official data products. Published consumer research supports the reported sampled behaviors. Publicly reported customer experience is a first-person public self-report. Observed public discussion records questions or advice without assuming it was acted on. Primary interview evidence and Primary survey evidence require authorized research and are absent here. Analyst inference labels synthesis; Unvalidated hypothesis labels proposals; Unknown means missing or incommensurate evidence. Publication dates can be approximate to year or month when the source supports only that precision. The source log includes geography, method, sample and limitations.

### Public voice coding

The unit is one distinct first-person behavior, decision or public question/advice. Several units may share a thread. Avoid duplicate points by the same visible contributor when practical and treat thread clusters as correlated. Include negative cases and satisficing with existing tools. Exclude removed material, promotional examples, identifying details, exact itineraries with personal information and unsupported safety conclusions. Keep no usernames, private messages, raw records or quotations. Assign each unit a primary theme and one or more pain IDs. English-language convenience sampling and search visibility overrepresent complex trips and users who post. Counts measure reading coverage only.

## Market rubric

Weights are applied only to evidenced dimensions. U means unknown and never contributes zero. Zero requires evidence of no signal for an evaluated dimension.

| Dimension | Weight | Scoring anchors from 0 to 5 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| demand | 25 | Zero reported comparable annual visits; positive under 0.5m; 0.5m–<3m; 3m–<10m; 10m–<25m; ≥25m. Use calendar 2025 official visitor/tourist volumes, disclose scope; mixed domestic/international figures remain contextual and unscored. Counts measure traffic, not customer demand. |
| complexity | 15 | Number of distinct evidenced constraints from zero to five: multi-stop ordering, separate modal handoff, schedule/connection dependency, access/payment constraints, changing plans. Scope only the cited journey. |
| season | 15 | Count documented timing signals up to five: reference period, monthly variation, regional variation, activity timing, weather uncertainty. |
| value | 15 | Count distinct evidenced traveler outcomes up to five: avoided effort, decision confidence, time saved, avoided cost, improved experience. Require market-specific experience; avoid extrapolating from unrelated trips. |
| data | 10 | Count publicly located candidate categories up to five: official visitor series, dated historical climate, forecast, advisory and transport/parking. This is existence screening; licensing, freshness and supported coverage remain for CP-0.4. |
| acquisition | 10 | Count distinct documented and attributable reachable channels up to five. No score for assumed audience or conversion. |
| opportunity | 10 | Count directly evidenced gaps in comparable market-specific surfaces up to five. No score from general feature absence or blocked testing. |

Calculate coverage = sum of scored weights. Normalized score = sum(weight × score) / coverage, out of five, rounded half up to two decimals. Sensitivity gives the full-weight lower/upper bounds by assigning 0/5 only as counterfactual extremes to all unknown weight. No overall placement from incomparable masks. Shortlist by complementary journey types, evidence quality and launch gates. Unknowns identify work for CP-0.4, not evidence of weakness.

## Review boundaries

No primary research, outreach, purchases, tracking, or participant data collection. The secondary package may enter review. The roadmap retains the 20–30 interview acceptance criterion. D24 authorizes a bounded secondary baseline with incomplete scores and zero primary research. Future fieldwork requires separate authorization and actual evidence must be reviewed before public V1.0.
