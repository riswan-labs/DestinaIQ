# Market analysis

| Market | Observed signal | Implication and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International leisure | UN Tourism reported 1.52 billion overnight international arrivals in 2025 (E01). | Large journey base. It includes business trips, repeated arrivals and other purposes. Independent planners at candidate destinations are an open subset. |
| Domestic leisure | Eurostat reports over 1.2 billion overnight trips by EU residents in 2025, roughly 70% domestic (E06). | Domestic trips may use alerts and dated local details, but channel, season and complexity must be validated separately. |
| Independent online research | Expedia tracked many pages and survey respondents cite price and trust (E03, E04). V04 and V08 illustrate multi-source research. | Some travelers benefit from consolidation; V58 and V61 show simple workflows already work. |
| Discovery and planning apps | Users report maps, sheets, airline apps and planners in parallel (V17, V27, V53, V62). | Test speed and evidence quality against tool combinations. Planning apps and destination pages are complementary for some journeys. |
| Safety and monitoring | Official guidance has geographic scope (E22), and DOT publishes disruption categories (E08). V22 and V25 give conflicting tracker anecdotes. | Validate opted-in relevance and freshness. Data provisioning, rights and coverage remain outstanding. |
| AI planning | Booking.com reports use alongside trust limits (E05). V38 reports useful drafts alongside incorrect hours. | Favor editable, source-backed drafts and require validation of consequential claims. |
| Creators and communities | Expedia reported social inspiration (E04); the CP-0.2 benchmark observed a creator feed (E23). | Test an acquisition channel separately from operating a contributor ecosystem. Post-launch rights, moderation and incentives remain open. |

WTTC's GDP and domestic travel spending measures (E07) describe travel's scale. They are not product market estimates. [Sizing model](MARKET_SIZING.md) · [Evidence](EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv)
