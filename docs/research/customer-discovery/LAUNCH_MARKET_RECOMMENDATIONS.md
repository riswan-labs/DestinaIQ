# Preliminary launch-market cohorts

The owner approved these research priorities for CP-0.4 planning under [D24](../../governance/DECISION_REGISTER.md#d24). Production launch selection remains open under D03.

- **Initial feasibility** — Japan exercises multi-city rail corridors, transfer schedules and regional seasonality. Singapore exercises time-sensitive arrival and dense urban transportation. Maldives exercises island and resort transfers, including day/night dependencies.
- **Sparse-data stress test** — French Polynesia exercises fragmented island records, inconsistent geographic scope and multi-leg air/boat connections.
- **Secondary feasibility** — Mauritius, Thailand, Indonesia, United Kingdom, Hawaii and Mexico provide additional route, island, regional and advisory scenarios.
- **Conditional candidates** — Seychelles, United Arab Emirates, Egypt, Qatar, Turkey, Greece, Italy, Switzerland, France and Puerto Rico retain their place in the long-term inventory. Advance their feasibility work as the specific evidence gaps warrant.

Nineteen markets have a scored signal and the United Arab Emirates remains wholly unscored. All 20 have unknown dimensions. Weighted coverage for Japan, Singapore, Maldives and French Polynesia is 80%, 50%, 65% and 65%, respectively. Their full-weight sensitivity bounds are 2.55–3.55, 1.65–4.15, 1.05–2.80 and 1.35–3.10. The overlap and unknown acquisition and opportunity dimensions preclude a defensible complete ranking.

[CP-0.4](../../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-04) must test geography, coverage, licensing and redistribution, freshness, authoritative issuer scope, units, operating costs and fallback behavior. [CP-0.8](../../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-08) retains privacy and safety review. Final selection also depends on actual customer demand and explicit owner release approval. The [coverage register](MARKET_COVERAGE.md), [original scorecard](LAUNCH_MARKET_SCORECARD_INITIAL.tsv), [updated scorecard](LAUNCH_MARKET_SCORECARD.tsv) and [rubric](METHODOLOGY.md#market-rubric) record the evidence and limits.
