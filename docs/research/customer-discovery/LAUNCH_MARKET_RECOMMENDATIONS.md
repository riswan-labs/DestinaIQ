# Preliminary launch-market cohorts

Recommend a first **feasibility cohort** of Japan, Singapore and Maldives. This is a research portfolio, not a ranking or publication decision. Japan tests multi-city seasonality and transit, Singapore tests a well-defined urban planning control, and Maldives tests transfers and island/coastal uncertainty. The first wave should only be as large as can meet approved category coverage and operating budgets. Limiting fully supported launch destinations lets the team verify provenance, freshness, missing data, alerts and costs before broadening. The searchable geography catalog can still resolve canonical places with explicit unsupported statuses.

- **Secondary expansion for validation** — French Polynesia, Mauritius, Thailand, Indonesia, United Kingdom, Hawaii and Mexico. This tests adjacent island workflows, route pacing, geographic scope and state-level advisory nuance. Market-specific rationale and dependencies are in the [scorecard](LAUNCH_MARKET_SCORECARD.tsv).
- **Conditional/deferred** — Seychelles, United Arab Emirates, Egypt, Qatar, Turkey, Greece, Italy, Switzerland, France and Puerto Rico. Missing comparable demand, suitable granular sources, or representative workflows limit confidence. These remain approved candidates from Design V3.

Eleven markets have at least one scored signal and nine have none. Every market has unscored dimensions. Only sampled signals were scored. Japan covers 80 weighted points, Singapore 35, and Maldives 65. On full-weight missing-data stress tests their respective ranges are 2.55–3.55, 1.10–4.35 and 1.05–2.80. Japan and Singapore's order can reverse under these bounds; the three different journey types justify the proposed portfolio. These extremes are sensitivity bounds, never used as scores. Hawaii's mixed-origin arrivals remain contextual only, and growth in Egypt or Seychelles is not a volume score. No acquisition or competitive-opportunity scores are assigned because comparable proof is absent.

CP-0.4 must verify two source tiers for critical fields where feasible, licensing and redistribution, geographic identity, authoritative issuer scope, units/time stamps, operating limits and per-supported-market costs. CP-0.8 must test safety interpretation, consent and privacy. No market qualifies as production-supported through this research.
