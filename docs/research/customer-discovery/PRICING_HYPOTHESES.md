# Pricing and willingness-to-pay hypotheses

Preserve Explorer free, Trip Pass USD 9.99–19.99, Plus USD 14.99/month, Pro USD 24.99/month and Enterprise custom as hypotheses. CP-0.2 observed WARNPOINT's USD 9.99 one-trip bundle and USD 14.99/month Pro, with different capability scope. Sitata quote-based assistance is a potential partner analog. Neither indicates DestinaIQ willingness to pay (E23). App discussions include actual self-reported purchases (V24, V64, V70), free sufficiency (V53, V61), and dissatisfaction after purchase (V65). These indicate heterogeneous trade-offs and potential subscription fatigue, with no prevalence or conversion estimate.

| Hypothesis | Test signal and falsifier |
| --- | --- |
| Explorer activates with comparable, cited search results | Observe completed compare/save against controls; friction or indistinguishable value falsifies |
| Trip Pass appeals to infrequent multi-stop customers | Compare bounded pass versus free and monthly; no-interest and former purchase are required measures |
| Monthly Plus supports frequent planners | Compare actual prior subscription episodes and repeat-trip use; one-trip use and high cancellation intent weaken it |
| Pro monitoring creates value where risk and changes matter | Test relevance and willingness against carrier notifications and free essentials; opt-out or no incremental action weakens it |
| Family/group value comes from shared decisions | Compare teamwork against notes with consent; no inference from party size |
| Seasonal retention differs from repeat research | Repeat-visit cohort and next-trip intent, using opted-in aggregation; avoid assuming year-round active subscribers |
| Enterprise is uncertain | Later interviews with buyers, workflow/rights and procurement evidence required |

[Pricing instrument](primary-research/PRICING_RESEARCH.md) handles price sensitivity and format testing. CP-0.4 should estimate source minimums, API and alert cost per monitored trip, storage, delivery, support, refunds, and free-tier subsidy. Later commercial decisions require observed price conversion and margin; no price or recurring plan is approved. [CP-0.2 pricing](../competitive-benchmark/PRICING_COMPARISON.md)

CP-0.4 carries forward provider licensing and costs, monitoring cost per trip, infrastructure cost, and commercial feasibility. Published competitor prices and gross tourist visits are context. Qualified TAM, SAM and SOM and DestinaIQ willingness to pay remain unknown.
