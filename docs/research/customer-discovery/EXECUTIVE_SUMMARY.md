# Executive summary

| Evidence or decision | Conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| Observed public customer experiences | 60 coded observations illustrate tool switching, uncertain schedules and transfer costs. Some travelers manage adequately with free maps, notes and airline alerts. Self-selected observations do not estimate prevalence. |
| Published consumer research | Published and official reports supply context for travel planning and tourism traffic. The [evidence register](EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv) contains 69 source records, including 15 additions from the bounded pass. Arrivals, lodging nights and guest records represent different populations. |
| Analyst inference | Date/place comparison, explainable destination scope and editable transfer-aware plans are plausible sources of value. Coverage and willingness to pay require direct testing. |
| Segment hypotheses | Independent international leisure travelers making complex, multi-destination or date-sensitive decisions are primary. Secondary and future segments and accessibility coverage appear in [customer segments](CUSTOMER_SEGMENTS.md). |
| Preliminary market recommendations | Japan, Singapore and Maldives are the first feasibility cohort. French Polynesia is a sparse-data stress test. [All 20 markets](MARKET_COVERAGE.md) remain candidates and have explicit evidence gaps. |
| Unvalidated pricing | Explorer is free in the hypothesis. Trip Pass, Plus, Pro and Enterprise price hypotheses remain open. Qualified TAM, SAM and SOM are unknown. See [pricing](PRICING_HYPOTHESES.md) and [sizing](MARKET_SIZING.md). |
| Unvalidated demand | Customer demand, willingness to pay, product-market fit and competitive differentiation remain unvalidated. The findings are exploratory and statistically nonrepresentative. |
| Outstanding primary work | Zero completed interviews and zero survey responses. The [follow-up register](PRIMARY_RESEARCH_FOLLOW_UP.md) retains the 20–30 interview objective and conditional 50–100 survey target. Actual primary evidence must reach the owner before public V1.0. |
| Outstanding feasibility | CP-0.4 owns source coverage, geographic fit, freshness, licensing, provider and monitoring costs and infrastructure assumptions. CP-0.8 retains privacy and safety validation. |
| Approved planning | D23 retains the 15 V1.0 capabilities. D24 authorizes bounded review and research-priority cohorts. D02 and D03 remain open, all seven [requirements proposals](REQUIREMENTS_IMPACT.md) await the owner, and CP-0.3 remains Under Review. |
