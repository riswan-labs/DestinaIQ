## Customer findings

The evidence register contains 54 source records, including 23 external institutional, published research or vendor/context sources, one accepted internal benchmark reference, and 30 public discussion/review source records. The public voice log contains 60 coded observations from self-selected public discussions and a storefront review. Twelve segments, 15 pains and nine jobs are considered.

Multiple sources show research/tool switching and itinerary constraints. Independently planning international leisure travelers with nontrivial routes and date choices are the proposed primary group. Couples, families, solo travelers, budget-conscious and activity-focused travelers are useful secondary hypotheses; premium differs by service needs, and accessibility is a cross-cutting research commitment. Corporate accounts, creators and advisors require separate validation. Prioritize comparing dates and places, verifiable destination scope and feasible transfers or editable routes. The contrary cases matter: some users prefer free notes/maps and airline alerts, and some welcome more structured itineraries. No prevalence or conversion is inferred.
