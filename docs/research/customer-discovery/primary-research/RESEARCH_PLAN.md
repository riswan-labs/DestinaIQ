# Primary research plan

**Draft. Zero participants.** The owner must authorize recruitment, channel use, retention, participant notice and any incentive before work begins. The roadmap requires 20–30 completed traveler interviews. Use 10–15 qualitative interviews as a first tranche and expand toward 20–30 for the existing gate. A separate 50–100 response convenience survey is exploratory. Neither is population-representative.

1. **Pilot (2–3 sessions within the first tranche).** Check questions and comprehension. Keep pilot findings separate when the instruments change. Use two interviewers or a moderator and note taker where feasible.
2. **Qualitative tranche.** Include independently planned international multi-stop leisure travelers with a trip in the last 12 months or upcoming trip, plus a deliberate contrast with simpler journeys. Mix couples, families, solo and budget-sensitive travelers and invite self-identified accessibility needs only as optional preferences without medical information. Include disinterested and free-tool-satisfied people. Recruit across owner audience and independent channels if separately approved. Use [interviews](INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md) and [concept cards](CONCEPT_TESTING.md).
3. **Survey and price exercise.** Seek 50–100 responses without representing them as statistically representative. Use quotas as diversity diagnostics and avoid forcing every respondent through every pricing question. Rotate at least concepts A and C and perform one bounded pricing exercise. See [survey](CUSTOMER_SURVEY.md) and [pricing](PRICING_RESEARCH.md).
4. **Decision review.** Analyze past behavior separately from intent, disconfirming cases by segment, and alternatives and price format. Use the [analysis template](ANALYSIS_TEMPLATE.md). Keep transcript-like material outside the public repo.

**Signals for a next pilot:** recurring concrete recent decisions with costly rework, examples across recruitment channels, higher aggregate comprehension of sourced options, and intentional reasons to return. **Failure signals:** free options are sufficient, participants cannot explain evidence or differentiate historical and forecast values, value vanishes when stopped from booking inside the concept, or alerts create distrust. Decide with descriptive counts and qualitative outcomes. Agree decision thresholds before fieldwork. Expand sampling if one channel or one destination dominates. Owner reviews D02/D03 and makes any scope and market choices.

**Resources:** planning estimate of 2–3 weeks after authorization, 20–30 moderator sessions of 20–30 minutes plus recruiting/analysis and a second reviewer. No dates, spend or participant count are committed. Owner nominates data steward, approves secure private storage, recruitment wording, incentive if any and withdrawal process.
