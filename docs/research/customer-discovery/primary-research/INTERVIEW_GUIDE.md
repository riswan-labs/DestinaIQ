# Customer interview guide

**Draft for owner authorization.** Target 25 minutes. Keep role, location and identity details coarse. Do not collect booking documents, identity data, employer or exact future travel plans.

**0–3 min · consent and context.** Read the [notice](PARTICIPATION_NOTICE.md). Ask if they want to proceed or skip any question. Confirm they planned a trip themselves in the last year. Ask broadly whether it was domestic/international and solo/shared. Avoid recording by default.

**3–12 min · last real trip.**

1. Walk me through the most recent trip you helped plan, from the idea to the first booking.
2. What tools did you actually open? What did you do in each, and what did you keep or reuse?
3. Roughly how much time did you spend researching? Which activity took the most and why?
4. Did you consider a different place or dates? How did you compare them? Did weather or seasons matter?
5. Tell me about something you trusted, something you checked again, and how you knew it was current.
6. What happened with airport transfers, daily transportation, safety guidance or itinerary changes? Were any of these easy?

**12–18 min · after booking and payment.**

7. Did you check for changes before or during travel? Walk me through a real alert or reason you checked. Which notifications did you ignore or disable?
8. Which preparation tasks did you reuse or redo? What was the outcome?
9. In the last 12 months, what did you actually buy for travel planning or monitoring, if anything? What did it cost approximately, when did you cancel or use it, and what free tool did you compare it with? Allow no purchases.
10. What part of the process would you keep exactly as it is?

**18–24 min · concepts.** Show two randomized neutral [cards](CONCEPT_TESTING.md), explain they are mockups, and ask the participant to explain each. Ask them to choose either or neither against their existing process and find or verify one fact. Ask if there is a real decision it would have changed, the reason it would not, and what they would do today.

**24–27 min · close.** Ask what we missed and whether the notes accurately summarize the account. Explain withdrawal process. Save only coded notes in the private store if authorized. Distinguish observed past behavior from speculation.
