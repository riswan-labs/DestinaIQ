# Concept testing cards

Present as separate research descriptions for an unbuilt product. Use neutral card order balanced across participants, same visual depth, and the same synthetic two-place and travel-month inputs.

| Card | Neutral description | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Destination intelligence | A page collects dated facts and links to sources for a place. Some fields may be unavailable. | Find the source and explain what its date means. |
| B Seasonal discovery | A view compares historical conditions by month and shows caveats. | Pick two months and explain why a historical condition is uncertain. |
| C Destination comparison | A table puts two or more shortlisted places on aligned criteria with unknown fields. | Choose based on your own criterion and identify a missing fact. |
| D Proactive monitoring | Optional summaries and critical messages about a saved trip. Users choose whether to subscribe or stop. | Decide when a mocked update merits attention; choose no alerts if appropriate. |
| E Personalized itinerary planning | An editable draft uses route and activity preferences with available dated evidence. | Move one activity and check travel time and opening hours. |

Each participant sees two or three cards; rotate and balance A/C at minimum. Start from their usual process and use comparable fixtures. Capture unaided comprehension, elapsed task time, verification errors, source tracing, preference (including neither), and negative evidence. Avoid motion or artwork polish confounds. No code, availability or reliability promise.
