# Customer survey draft

**Unpublished.** Allow 5–8 minutes. Show the [participation notice](PARTICIPATION_NOTICE.md) first. Continue only on voluntary agreement. All questions may be skipped. Do not request names or contact details in the response form.

1. Trips you helped plan in the last 12 months: none, one, two to three, four or more, unsure.
2. Most recent trip: domestic, international, both, no trip. Purpose: leisure, work, visiting others, mixed, other. Party: alone, couple, family, group, other.
3. What did you do yourself? Choose all: select destination, compare dates, book transport/lodging, plan activities, monitor changes, none.
4. Which tools did you use? Search, map, official information, notes/sheet, carrier, travel organizer, advisor, AI chat, other, none. Ask order used if remembered.
5. Approximate active planning time: under one hour, 1–3, 4–8, more than 8, do not recall, not applicable.
6. What caused rework, if anything? Pick up to three shuffled options: choosing dates, comparing places, current information, safety, transfer/parking, cost, pacing, changes after booking, packing, none, other.
7. How often did you confirm important information against another source? Never, occasionally, often, every time, do not recall. What sources did you use? Optional.
8. Prioritize up to three concept cards, plus “none of these”. Ask which actual decision each might support. Mark as intent.
9. In the past year did you buy a travel planning or monitoring tool? No, one-off, subscription, both, uncertain. Optional broad price band and reason. Do not include travel bookings.
10. Randomized [pricing question](PRICING_RESEARCH.md). Include free existing tool, not interested, and cannot tell.
11. Preferred access if any: web, iOS, Android, email updates, other, no interest. Optional feedback without identifying details.

Thank respondent. For withdrawal and contact, use owner-approved instructions in the notice. Do not attach promotions or recruit through this draft.
