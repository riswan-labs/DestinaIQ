# Draft participation notice

**For owner and appropriate privacy review before use.** Participation is voluntary. The purpose is to understand how travelers plan, choose information, prepare and make purchase decisions. You can skip questions or stop at any time. Taking part does not affect access to anything and does not imply a purchase.

If approved, the team will collect anonymous or coded survey answers and notes from the conversation. It will ask about broad trip context, tools and behavior. Please do not share passport details, booking documents, exact itineraries, medical information or other sensitive identifiers. Recording is off by default; any recording requires separate advance consent.

The research team uses approved data to improve planning and evaluate hypotheses. Only appropriately aggregated or anonymized findings may be published after owner authorization. Raw notes, responses and any private contact linkage remain outside the public repository with access limited to approved researchers. The proposed retention is 60 days for coded notes after synthesis, with recruitment contacts removed sooner once follow-up closes. The owner must approve the actual schedule, access and disposal prior to collection.

To withdraw or request deletion, use **[owner-approved private contact to be supplied before recruitment]** and provide your study code if you have one. Explain any limit once findings are de-identified and no longer retrievable to an individual. No claim of legal compliance is made by this draft.
