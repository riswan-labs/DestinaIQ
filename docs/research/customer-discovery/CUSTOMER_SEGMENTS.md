# Customer segmentation

The groups overlap. Frequency descriptions are hypotheses, and segment sizes and purchasing rates are unknown. Initial participant quotas should favor independent multi-stop international leisure planners, with counterexamples from simple-trip and already-satisfied travelers. Parents and solo travelers should be intentionally represented. Accessibility is a cross-cutting research priority. Creator and agency workflows are separately gated post-launch.
