# Jobs-to-be-Done hypotheses

Rank by three pre-declared signals, each 0–2: evidence breadth (none, one channel, corroborated channels), consequence (small convenience, rework, trip or trust impact), V1.0 fit (outside, partial, direct). Scores rank research priorities, not customer measurements. Ties prioritize nearer booking decisions.
