# Research limitations

- **Primary evidence** — Zero interviews and zero survey responses. No recruitment, accounts, messages, purchases, private participant data or outreach. Public paraphrases are secondary evidence. The roadmap gate requires 20–30 interviews and 20 scored markets. All markets are represented, and every market has unsupported dimensions. D24 authorizes independent review of a bounded secondary baseline. Primary evidence and explicit owner approval of limitations are required before public V1.0.
- **Selection and geography** — English-language public convenience sampling concentrates on Japan, Hawaii and general planning communities. Public replies share threads and self-selection. French Polynesia source evidence includes indexed excerpts where direct reading was limited. Segment prevalence and accessibility-specific preferences are unknown.
- **Measurement** — Surveys by travel businesses cover their selected respondents. Official arrivals use different definitions and do not equal qualified unique customers. Self-reported successful and unsuccessful purchases do not establish willingness to pay.
- **Scope** — Do not infer safety from forum experiences, forecasts from climatology, traffic-based demand from country volumes, or commercial licensing from a public URL. Full feasibility, coverage, rights, privacy safeguards, delivered alerts and quantitative customer outcomes remain open.
- **Recommendations** — The feasibility shortlist, positions, proposed counts and effort estimates are research choices. None approves a market, price, architectural commitment or product change. D23 remains in force.
