# Primary customer research follow-up

| Control | Recorded obligation |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Test customer demand, workflow correctness and time saved, trust, competitive differentiation against free tools, and willingness to pay in concept and prototype tasks. |
| Responsible owner | Project owner authorizes the work, appoints the research lead and privacy reviewer, and signs off on the evidence and limitations before public V1.0. |
| Participants | Independent international leisure travelers with a recent or planned multi-destination, complicated or date-sensitive trip. Include return and occasional travelers, price-sensitive and premium travelers, and varied accessibility needs. Distinguish actual decision-makers from observers. |
| Recruitment | After separate authorization, use opt-in approved public calls or consented research panels, screen for trip profile, seek varied routes and budgets, and log channel bias. No copying account data, unsolicited outreach or participant recruitment under this assignment. |
| Interview target and actuals | 20–30 completed traveler interviews. Completed primary interviews: 0. |
| Survey target and actuals | 50–100 exploratory responses, subject to owner approval. Completed primary surveys: 0. |
| Validation actuals | Validated willingness to pay: No. Validated customer demand: No. Product-market fit and differentiation unvalidated. |
| Instruments | Use the [research plan](primary-research/RESEARCH_PLAN.md), [guide](primary-research/INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md), [survey](primary-research/CUSTOMER_SURVEY.md), [concept protocol](primary-research/CONCEPT_TESTING.md), [pricing tasks](primary-research/PRICING_RESEARCH.md), [recruitment drafts](primary-research/RECRUITMENT_TEMPLATES.md), [notice](primary-research/PARTICIPATION_NOTICE.md) and [analysis template](primary-research/ANALYSIS_TEMPLATE.md). |
| Consent and handling | Obtain informed opt-in consent and separate optional recording permission. Approve purpose, access, retention and deletion before fieldwork. Store contact details, consent, recordings and raw survey responses outside the public repository with restricted access. Commit only appropriately anonymized aggregated findings after authorization. |
| Analysis | Predefine workflow measures and denominators, rotate concept order, compare free-tool alternatives, separate interview behavior from survey intent, code confirming and disconfirming results, and document sample bias and uncertainty. |
| Decision outputs | Evidence and limitations memo, task accuracy and time comparisons, segment and positioning results, observed alternatives, price-response ranges, and recommendations for D02, D03 and D10. |
| Dependencies and status | Await separate owner authorization, reviewed consent and storage, named lead, accessible prototype and recruitment plan. Authorized work may run in parallel with later engineering. |
| Acceptance criteria | Review actual completed interviews and any authorized survey, coded evidence and contrary cases, protocol deviations, denominators and unresolved gaps. Retain the original interview target. Any residual limitation requires explicit project-owner approval. |
| Readiness and completion gates | Carry owner, instruments, risks and outstanding targets to [CP-0.9](../../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-09). Review primary results with usability at [CP-6.7](../../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-67), record rollout evidence at [CP-6.11](../../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-611), use [CP-6.13](../../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-613) if applicable, and require owner review of actual evidence and explicit approval of limitations at [CP-6.14](../../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-614) before public V1.0 launch. |

[D24](../../governance/DECISION_REGISTER.md#d24) permits CP-0.3 acceptance as a bounded secondary baseline. D02 and D03 remain open.
