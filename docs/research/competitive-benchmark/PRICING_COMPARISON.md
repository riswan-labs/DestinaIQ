Design V3 hypothesizes free Explorer, USD 9.99–19.99 trip pass, USD 14.99 Plus, USD 24.99 Pro and custom enterprise. WARNPOINT’s USD 9.99 bundle and USD 14.99 Pro show public packaging at two overlapping price points. Product scope, alert data, coverage and customer value differ. Sitata bundles protection and assistance, so a quote would be an unsuitable direct anchor for unbundled DestinaIQ pricing. Public prices do not measure demand, margin, support cost, or willingness to pay. [Design V3 pricing hypotheses](../../product/DESIGN.md#18-unit-economics-and-pricing)

- CP-0.3 proposal: test who pays, per-trip versus recurring preferences, willingness to pay for fresh information, and whether weekly alert value differs by trip type.
- CP-0.4 proposal: estimate licensing, updates, geocoding and messaging costs per monitored trip, including quiet periods and surges.
- CP-6.10 and CP-11.11 proposals: validate cost budgets, refunds, tax, usage and partner terms before any commercial approval. D10 and D22 remain open.
