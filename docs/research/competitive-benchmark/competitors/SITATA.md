# Sitata

Official site https://www.sitata.com/. Research observed 2026-11-27 (America/Los_Angeles). Times and citations appear in the [evidence register](../EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv).

## Positioning and coverage

Travel protection, assistance and alerts for insured and independent travelers, plus platform distribution for travel partners. Vendor claims monitoring in 220+ countries; eligibility and detail vary by market and plan.

## Product and provenance claims

FAQ documents email-forwarded imports, offline saved details, trip tracking and community reports. Product docs describe assistance and third-party data-triggered payouts; app and partner workflows were not exercised.

## Observed access and functionality

Consumer website rendered marketing and quote entry; separate app availability is evidenced by official distribution sources. Account and entitlement required for meaningful saved-trip/insurance testing are not established for every region. A regional UAE iOS listing describes one-time PIN sign-in and entry features; other regions and Android were not tested (SI06).

## Commercial and privacy representations

Free-download app with documented basic safety features; premium tools and quote-based protection. Assistance-only annual/roaming products for partners; exact pricing and discounts unverified. Privacy policy describes plan-related data handling and additional terms.

Evidence SI01, SI02, SI03, SI04, SI05, SI06. See the [scenario log](../SCENARIO_RESULTS.tsv) and [pricing comparison](../PRICING_COMPARISON.md).
