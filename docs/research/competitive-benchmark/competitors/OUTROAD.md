# Outroad

Official site https://getoutroad.com/. Research observed 2026-11-27 (America/Los_Angeles). Times and citations appear in the [evidence register](../EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv).

## Positioning and coverage

Local travel-risk guidance for travelers deciding where to stay or how to move. Browser-based desktop/mobile. Advertises 90+ countries and 4,000 cities/neighborhoods; inventory totals not independently checked.

## Product and provenance claims

Promotes incident, risk and insight map layers, neighborhood assessment, scams and transport guidance. Policy identifies collection and disclosure categories; no source assessment, scoring validation or freshness audit performed.

## Observed access and functionality

Rendered public map with Incidents, Safety and Insight controls. Signed-out search is advertised, saved destinations and alerts use an account. Matched neighborhood outputs are stated in the scenario log if retrieved. Mexico City search rendered a match, then map controls. Both target neighborhood names appeared as suggestions (OR04, OR05). Neighborhood detail was unverified.

## Commercial and privacy representations

Free search and free account advertised. Paid tiers, enterprise prices, billing periods, trial and revenue model not established. No private data or subscriptions used.

Evidence OR01, OR02, OR03, OR04, OR05. See the [scenario log](../SCENARIO_RESULTS.tsv) and [pricing comparison](../PRICING_COMPARISON.md).
