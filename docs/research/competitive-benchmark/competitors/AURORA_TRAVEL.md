# Aurora Travel

Official site https://auroratravelapp.com/. Research observed 2026-11-27 (America/Los_Angeles). Times and citations appear in the [evidence register](../EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv).

## Positioning and coverage

Community-first discovery, safety and trip planning for travelers. Vendor advertises global guide counts, examples for Japan, UAE, Egypt, Indonesia and Maldives, web and iOS/Android distribution.

## Product and provenance claims

Advertises location-tagged creator media and expenses, AI itineraries, three government advisory sources, local alerts, maps and social tools. Coverage claims and example assistant replies are marketing.

## Observed access and functionality

The signed-out web onboarding rendered. The app shell initially required JavaScript. App install, premium tools, moderation and notification delivery were not evaluated. Skipping onboarding opened Studio with country and currency fields. A separate signed-out country safety page showed named authorities and a checked date (AU04, AU05).

## Commercial and privacy representations

Vendor download page says free download. Paid plans, terms by geography, paid trials and revenue model not established. No service-specific privacy policy verified in this pass; location-alert permission is a vendor representation.

Evidence AU01, AU02, AU03, AU04–AU06. See the [scenario log](../SCENARIO_RESULTS.tsv) and [pricing comparison](../PRICING_COMPARISON.md).
