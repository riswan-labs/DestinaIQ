# WARNPOINT Trip Watch

Official site https://warnpoint.com/trip-watch. Research observed 2026-11-27 (America/Los_Angeles). Times and citations appear in the [evidence register](../EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv).

## Positioning and coverage

Travel add-on to a broader open-source risk product, pitched to travelers before and during trips and to professional intelligence consumers. Markets city/country monitoring, web and push on supported desktops and phones.

## Product and provenance claims

Vendor describes news and government advisory inputs, corroboration, derivative estimates, daily monitoring, printable brief, quiet hours and geographic notices. Published method distinguishes estimates from official advice.

## Observed access and functionality

Reviewed public product, plan, methodology and terms pages. Restricted competitor-output evaluation under published terms; retained no risk outputs or product scores. No monitoring or payment transaction attempted.

## Commercial and privacy representations

Public alert product has free discovery and paid USD single-trip and subscription tiers. Custom quoted packages. The privacy policy describes options for location, browser storage, push and account deletion; compliance unverified.
