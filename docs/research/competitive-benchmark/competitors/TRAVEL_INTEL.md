# Travel Intel

Official site https://www.travelintel.tech/. Research observed 2026-11-27 (America/Los_Angeles). Times and citations appear in the [evidence register](../EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv).

## Positioning and coverage

Decision support for independent travelers deciding where and when to go. Public web and multi-country index with city/island examples. No installed app verified.

## Product and provenance claims

Seasonal atlas, forecasts, AQI, events, official-advisory guardrail and side-by-side comparison. NASA POWER historical climate, named providers and a method explanation. The published State Department safety scope is limited to higher advisory levels.

## Observed access and functionality

Public no-account browsing and demo-like landing pages. Observed Tokyo, Kyoto, Bali and Maldives climate sections. Published compare scope is two or three, even though S08 requests four. Chrome rendered a Bora-Bora suggestion and Dubai's advisory guardrail (TI11, TI12). The follow-up rendered Bora Bora's sourced historical climate (TI15). A separate Tokyo/Kyoto control rendered a two-slot comparison and its uncertainty caveats (TI16).

## Commercial and privacy representations

Public access observed. Paid tier, trial, billing frequency, geographic restrictions and commercial model beyond public web are unknown. Policy describes local storage and cookie choices; deletion or security controls were not tested.
