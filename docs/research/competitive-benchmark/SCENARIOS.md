# Standardized travel scenarios

Dates use local destination time. All inputs are synthetic. When a surface accepts only a destination or month, record that exact slice and preserve the full scenario reference. Verify all requested outputs only when supported.

## S01

**French Polynesia seasonal intelligence**

Inputs — Bora Bora, 2027-08-09 to 2027-08-16; two adults; swimming, snorkeling and lagoon excursions; arrive BOB; no special needs.

Expected checks — Resolve the island; show historical August rain and temperatures, seasonal crowding, marine suitability, transfer and accommodation areas, advisories and source dates.

## S02

**Dubai luxury vacation**

Inputs — Dubai, 2027-12-05 to 2027-12-12; two adults; USD 6,000 excluding flights; beaches, fine dining and culture; arrive DXB; rental-car option.

Expected checks — Identify weather and peak-season effects, attractions, transfers, transit and parking, customs, safety, personalized activities and estimated per-category costs.

## S03

**Japan cherry blossom travel**

Inputs — Tokyo → Kyoto → Osaka, 2027-03-27 to 2027-04-05; one adult; rail only; cherry blossoms prioritized; USD 2,500 excluding flights.

Expected checks — Compare location-specific bloom windows, travel dates and uncertainty; distinguish history from prediction, show intercity transit, crowds and price trade-offs.

## S04

**Seven-day Bali itinerary**

Inputs — Bali, 2027-07-12 to 2027-07-19; two adults; USD 1,600 excluding flights; Ubud and Sanur; beaches, culture and rest; ≤2 hours scheduled transport per day.

Expected checks — Produce an editable, source-linked itinerary; check geographic order, hours, travel time, weather and budget; replace one beach with a cultural stop.

## S05

**International multi-stop journey**

Inputs — Seattle → Doha → Cairo → Dubai, 2027-10-02 to 2027-10-12; one synthetic US passport nationality; Doha airside connection of 3 hours; Cairo stay October 3–7; Dubai October 7–12; no document uploads.

Expected checks — Preserve segment continuity, organize reservations, distinguish transit and entry requirements where supported, show airport/transfer details, briefings and offline behavior.

## S06

**Travel disruption**

Inputs — Simulated SEA→DOH arrival 16:00 AST on 2027-10-03 slips to 19:15; onward DOH→CAI departs 18:00 AST; hotel check-in 22:00 EET and next-day tour at 08:00 local; no live flight identifiers.

Expected checks — Trace cascading impacts, alternatives and approvals; inspect notification controls and emergency help. Log live delivery only if directly observed using an authorized workflow.

## S07

**Accommodation and neighborhood assessment**

Inputs — Mexico City, 2027-05-10 to 2027-05-14; compare in Roma Norte near Plaza Río de Janeiro and Centro Histórico near Zócalo; public landmarks only; evening walking and transit.

Expected checks — Check neighborhood granularity, maps, healthcare, scam/transit and parking information, evidence recency and uncertainty; avoid demographic proxies or unsupported safety labels.

## S08

**Budget and destination comparison**

Inputs — Maldives, Seychelles, Bali and Mauritius; 2027-09-06 to 2027-09-13; two adults; USD 2,800 excluding international flights; swimming, local food, public transport when available.

Expected checks — Compare seasonal suitability, accommodation/activity/transfer cost ranges, currency and preference trade-offs. Separate listed, estimated and observed prices.
