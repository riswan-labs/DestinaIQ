# Competitive gap analysis

The [scenario log](SCENARIO_RESULTS.tsv) and [matrix](CAPABILITY_MATRIX.tsv) establish the comparison scope. The strongest observed slices were Travel Intel’s attributable climate tables and safety override, Aurora’s rendered creator artifacts and dated advisory index, TripGuard’s city resolution and scan entry, and Outroad’s city resolution leading to map controls. No delivery/reliability ranking was established.

- **Demonstrated contrast.** Travel Intel’s published two-/three-place control is narrower than FR-014’s four-place plan. Its observed approximate Japan bloom notice is useful while leaving city-specific bloom timing and validation open. Aurora already presents creator posts with cost fields in public; simple posting plus currency is weak differentiation. TI04–TI08, AU04.
- **Potential depth beyond launch.** WARNPOINT offers a documented daily cadence, same-page advisory comparisons, printable briefs and quiet hours. Sitata describes flight updates, confirmation forwarding and assistance; Aurora describes mobile offline tools and community features. These are market signals; end-to-end behavior remains unverified. WP01, WP04, SI02, SI03, AU03.
- **Candidate gaps in Design V3.** Sitata’s published parametric payout mechanic and WARNPOINT’s branded connectivity bundle extend beyond DestinaIQ’s planned direct scope. Consider specialist partnerships where there is demand and a governed commercial model. Defer underwriting, claims operations and connectivity resale. SI03, SI05, WP02.
- **Proposed baseline expectations.** Dated government evidence, readable seasonal context, explicit uncertainty, and reasonably easy place selection are recurring patterns. Validate user salience before treating breadth, day-level forecasts or AI synthesis as table stakes. TI02, TI11, AU05, TG03, OR04.
- **Main launch risk.** Candidate-island coverage, freshness/corrections and trustworthy transport and itinerary constraints matter more than advertised market counts. Maintain V1.0’s planned capabilities while validating representative markets, verification evidence and provider economics. Assess any scope change separately with the owner. TI02, TI03, TI11, AU05.
- **High-cost and deferred scope.** Real-time flight coverage, permissioned sharing, regulated assistance, neighborhood risk and live parking add cost, safety or data-rights obligations. Keep authoritative staging and use partner diligence for assistance. Moderated creator and vault/social features retain separate approval gates. SI03, SI04, WP03, OR01, OR02.
