# Executive summary

The public benchmark supports keeping DestinaIQ’s **planned V1.0 focus** while validating whether customers value trustworthy, dated information across search, climate, advisories and basic planning. It provides no evidence for expanding launch scope. CP-0.2 is **Blocked at review entry**. Public research covers 6 competitors, 8 scenarios, and 48 combinations (8 partial hands-on, 18 documentation-only, 8 restricted, 14 untested). No full scenario was verified.

Travel Intel is the closest observed seasonal and provenance comparator. Aurora demonstrates a public advisory index and a destination-tagged creator feed. Outroad is the most relevant local discovery comparator; its city and both neighborhood suggestions and map controls were observed. Neighborhood detail remained unverified. TripGuard’s staged scan entry overlaps personalized pre-trip intelligence, but its completed output and mobile parity remain unknown. Sitata and WARNPOINT are especially relevant to post-booking assistance and monitoring, supported here by public documentation rather than live delivery.

Travel Intel had the clearest source presentation in the measured climate slices. The three matching slices scored 5/5 on one dimension, covering only 30/800 available weight across its eight scenarios. No product qualifies for an overall ranking. Travel Intel’s published comparison limit is narrower than DestinaIQ’s planned four-place view; Tokyo’s blossom notice uses an approximate shared window. Outroad’s map text did not establish local data coverage. The TripGuard report was still compiling at the last observation. See the [scorecard](SCORECARD.md) and [scenario log](SCENARIO_RESULTS.tsv).

Differentiation evidence supports investing in claim-level attribution and honest climate/activity uncertainty as quality goals. Competitors already show pieces of both, so neither is proven unique. Digital Twin, What-If, deep parking data, readiness workflows, temporal corrections and proactive alert quality remain hypotheses. Defer live parking, continuous alert expansion, identity vault, social and regulated assistance until data, user value, costs and privacy are validated. Documented USD trip-pass prices overlap existing hypotheses, but list prices provide no demand or margin evidence.

Owner review is needed for launch-scope reaffirmation, further access, every proposed requirement change and any additional test scope. CP-0.3 should eventually test decision time, trust, interest in cohorts and willingness to pay. CP-0.4 should test island/place resolution, authoritative source coverage, terms, and cost per monitored trip. These are follow-up proposals. [Access and remaining gaps](ACCESS_LIMITATIONS.md)
