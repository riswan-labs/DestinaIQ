# Executive summary

CP-0.2 is **Accepted** under the [owner-authorized bounded research policy](METHODOLOGY.md#owner-authorized-evidence-closure). The six-competitor, eight-scenario log has 48 combinations: **0 fully verified**, 8 partial hands-on, 18 documentation-only, 8 restricted, 14 untested. None of the additional controls completed all checks for a standardized scenario. This is a preliminary planning baseline.

## Verified observations

Travel Intel rendered a Bora Bora destination page with August historical normals, the NASA POWER attribution, a 2001–2020 reference period and a warning that typical conditions are separate from forecasts. Its comfort method excludes crowds, prices and festivals. A separate Tokyo/Kyoto control rendered a two-slot comparison and caveats. It did not exercise the four destinations in S08. TripGuard’s signed-out Seattle-to-Dubai control reached a completed report with source labels and practical sections. The control omitted dates and the page displayed November weather, leaving the original December scenario and factual accuracy unverified. Aurora’s public assistant exposed an input, but no dated Bali itinerary, editable result or source citations were captured. Outroad rendered public map layers and both target neighborhood names during the selection attempts; source dates, precise geographic attribution and defensible safety conclusions remained unavailable. ## Vendor claims, prices and untested workflows

Sitata’s publicly illustrated alert, assistance and travel-buddy workflows are vendor representations. No claims, assistance or live delivery were exercised. WARNPOINT’s public feature and pricing material remains eligible for qualitative research. Output benchmarking was excluded under its terms. Its published USD 9.99 trip bundle and USD 14.99/month Pro overlap existing DestinaIQ price hypotheses, with no evidence of comparable value, checkout or willingness to pay. [Pricing comparison](PRICING_COMPARISON.md) · [Access limitations](ACCESS_LIMITATIONS.md)

No overall competitor ranking is supported. Travel Intel’s score is limited to its observed climate source-transparency slices and covers a small fraction of possible scenario weight. No accuracy, latency or end-to-end product grade follows from that score. DestinaIQ is a planned product and has no hands-on score. [Weighted scorecard](SCORECARD.md)

## Approved launch planning decision

**Retain the existing 15 V1.0 launch capabilities without expansion.** The evidence supports keeping attributable destination facts, historical periods, uncertainty and official advisories central to V1.0. The 13 recorded dispositions concern research or potential refinements. Specification changes require their own approval. Customer demand, provider feasibility, licensing, unit economics and production readiness remain unvalidated. The project owner approved this planning decision under [D23](../../governance/DECISION_REGISTER.md#d23), subject to later discovery, licensing, cost, privacy and production-readiness validation.
