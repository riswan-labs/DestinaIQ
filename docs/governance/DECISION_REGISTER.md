# Decision register

All items are **Open**. The checkpoint indicates the planned decision point or first validation gate. Resolution requires evidence and recorded approval. Reservation and commercialization decisions are prepared during discovery and resolved at their respective gates.

| ID | Decision | Required evidence | Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | Hands-on competitor benchmark | Six attempts with screenshots, prices, weights, tested coverage and access blockers. | [CP-0.2](../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-02) |
| D02 | Customer discovery and product hypotheses | 20–30 interviews and prototype usability tests with research time and willingness-to-pay evidence. | [CP-0.2](../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-02) |
| D03 | Initial destination coverage and launch sign-off | Validate each candidate and publish searchable versus supported catalog, coverage and owner sign-off. | [CP-0.2](../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-02) |
| D04 | Provider licensing and attribution | Record commercial use, storage limits, redistribution, territory, rates and attribution per source. | [CP-0.2](../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-02) |
| D05 | Commercial API costs and provider selection | Obtain quotes, assumptions and usage sensitivity across 1k, 10k and 100k active users. | [CP-0.2](../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-02) |
| D06 | AWS versus Azure | Compare cost, service fit, reliability, operations and recovery constraints in an ADR. | [CP-0.2](../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-02) |
| D07 | Data retention and deletion policy | Approve category and backup TTLs, legal holds and retention verification. | [CP-0.2](../engineering/ROADMAP.md#cp-02) |
